## Research & Opportunities

I selectively consider students for Bachelor's and Master's theses, research internships, and research assistant positions.

A strong mathematical background is essential. My research involves inherently difficult mathematical problems requiring theoretical depth, independent reasoning, and intellectual rigor.

If you are comfortable with mathematical abstraction and tackling problems without predefined solutions, feel free to reach out.
